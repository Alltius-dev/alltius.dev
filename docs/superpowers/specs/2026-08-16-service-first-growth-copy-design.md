# AIULLMA service-first growth copy

Date: 2026-08-16  
Status: approved direction; awaiting written-spec review

## Objective

Reposition the AIULLMA institutional site around revenue growth while preserving its corporate credibility and B1 “Midnight Cobalt” identity. The message must present AIULLMA as an ongoing managed-service company in English, Brazilian Portuguese and neutral Latin American Spanish. Technology, implementation and dedicated infrastructure are the means; revenue growth, operating capacity, better decisions and stronger margins are the intended business outcomes.

## Approved positioning

AIULLMA builds, operates and optimizes the technology layer that supports growth. Its ongoing service combines automation, systems, AI agents, business intelligence and dedicated infrastructure. The copy may state that these capabilities help companies identify opportunities, make better decisions, serve more demand and grow. It must not guarantee revenue, ROI, conversion rates, savings or margins.

## Message hierarchy

1. Revenue growth is the primary promise.
2. Managed technology and AI services are the delivery model.
3. Better decisions, more operating capacity and scalable economics explain the value.
4. Building, operating and optimizing explain how AIULLMA works.
5. Dedicated infrastructure is the enabling foundation, not the product headline.
6. Corporate identity, policies and direct contact support diligence and verification.

## Home-page structure

The visual structure and existing routes remain unchanged. The English and Portuguese home pages will use this narrative order:

1. Hero: revenue-growth promise, ongoing service and economic differentiation.
2. Company: AIULLMA as a managed technology-and-AI service company.
3. Capabilities framed as business outcomes.
4. Operating model framed as build, operate and optimize.
5. Scale economics and margin protection.
6. Corporate diligence.
7. Growth-oriented contact invitation.

## Spanish localization architecture

Spanish uses neutral Latin American wording and the BCP 47 language tag `es-419`. English remains the default and `x-default` destination. The site never redirects automatically by IP, country or browser language.

| English | Portuguese | Spanish — Latin America |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | `/pt/` | `/es/` |
| `/contact` | `/pt/contato` | `/es/contacto` |
| `/privacy` | `/pt/privacidade` | `/es/privacidad` |
| `/terms` | `/pt/termos` | `/es/terminos` |
| `/data-deletion` | `/pt/exclusao-de-dados` | `/es/eliminacion-de-datos` |

Each route publishes a canonical URL and alternates for `en`, `pt-BR`, `es-419` and `x-default`. The sitemap contains all 15 localized URLs. The root layout emits `lang="es-419"` for Spanish pages.

The current single alternate-language link becomes a compact three-language selector on desktop and mobile: **English · Português · Español**. The current language is rendered as the active item with `aria-current="page"`; the other two items link to the equivalent localized route. The selector does not change the B1 visual system or add client-side state.
